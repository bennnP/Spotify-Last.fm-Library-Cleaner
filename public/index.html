Frequently Asked Questions

Does this remove tracks automatically?

No. The app only removes tracks after you review the candidates and confirm the action.

What data is stored?

This local version keeps tokens and analysis results in memory while the server runs. Read the privacy policy before connecting an account.

Why can a large export take a while?

History files and Spotify collections are processed in the background. The progress area updates while work is running.
